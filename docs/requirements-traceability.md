# Requirements and constraints traceability

This is a proposed enterprise acceptance baseline, not stakeholder sign-off. **Implemented** means present in source and locally testable; it does not mean deployed or production-certified. **Proposed** means work remains. Owners below are accountable roles to assign before pilot. Targets are initial negotiation points, not measured results or promises.

The value hypothesis is that faster, evidence-backed inventory decisions reduce stockout exposure and planner effort without increasing unsafe transfers. Parallel analysis is an enabling capability; realized margin, service and adoption determine value. Promotion planning remains a second scenario and should follow inventory validation, rather than expand the initial pilot.

Decision IDs resolve in the [decision log](decision-log.md). Gate and metric definitions resolve in the [delivery roadmap](delivery-roadmap.md). Component paths are relative to the repository root.

## Maintaining the baseline

The product owner maintains requirements with data, security, operations and finance owners. Each material change must update its decision, component, test/evaluation evidence, residual risk and outcome measure together. Gate packs record named risk owners, severity, mitigation, due date and explicit acceptance; unknown or unowned critical risks block progression. Use the [threat model](threat-model.md) for security detail and [production readiness](production-readiness.md) for outstanding technical checks. Tests listed above establish prototype properties only; proposed controls require new implementation and evidence.
